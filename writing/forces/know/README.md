The **know** door holds the word-to-world forces: they make words answer to the world, so the reader ends up *knowing* something.
Their shared virtue is fidelity; their shared temptation is sliding into one another, because they all traffic in true statements and differ only in what the truth is *for*.

- **[orient](orient/README.md)** — situate the reader in a whole (`locate`).
  Where am I, why does this concern me, where next.
- **[explain](explain/README.md)** — build the reader's model of *why* (`model`).
- **[describe](describe/README.md)** — state the facts exhaustively, neutrally (`state`).

The dividing question: does the document *change* the reader (orient reshapes their map, explain their model → organize by the reader) or merely *serve* them (describe → organize by the subject)?
If a reference starts answering *why*, it has walked through the wrong door.

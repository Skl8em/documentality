The **do** door holds the world-to-world forces addressed to a reader who will *act*: they aim at the world through the reader's doing. Read the door as a **deontic gradient** — how strongly the document binds the reader:

- **[instruct](instruct/README.md)** — *here is how* to do a settled task, neutrally (`state`). Steps, preconditions, verification.
- **[teach](teach/README.md)** — *bring the learner along* until they are able and confident (`enable`).
- **[recommend](recommend/README.md)** — *you should* do this, non-binding (`state`). Advice with its tradeoff and its exceptions.

The next notch on the gradient — *you must*, binding — is `mandate`, which lives behind the **govern & record** door because it institutes a norm rather than helping a reader act. Keep the gradient visible: a reader must always be able to tell whether they are being shown a procedure, advised, or commanded.

The working plan, revisable. It names the **construction phases** (how *we* build the system) and maps them onto the two **product horizons** of [`foundation.md`](foundation.md): Phase I (an operational system) and Phase II (a computational model).
Phases 01–05 are past and recorded in [`phases/`](phases/README.md); this roadmap looks forward from 06.

| Phase | Aim | Serves | Key outputs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **06 — Refoundation & front-end** *(current)* | Absorb the Ferraris-grounded theory; lay the foundation flat; name the plan; record the founding decisions. | I | `foundation.md`, this roadmap, phase-06 ADRs |

| **09 — Reconcile governance & the frontmatter schema** | Make the catalogue the machine interface: the schema (fields, allowed values, validation) reconciled to the refounded model; `docs/` records realigned. | I → II | schema spec; validation rules |
| **10 — Computational model** | Generate reader surfaces from the catalogue; AI instructions, hooks, validation/generation. | II | generators; hooks; AI operating instructions |

Verification runs *within* each phase, not as a final gate: a phase closes only when its outputs pass their own checks (links, schema validity, self-consistency).

Two standing debts, tracked so they are not forgotten: migrate the nine not-yet-built force folders from `_legacy/` (folded into Phase 08), and retire `_legacy/` once migration completes.

This roadmap is maintained: as a phase closes, its row is marked done and its records freeze in `phases/`.
Changing the plan is a recorded decision, not a silent edit.

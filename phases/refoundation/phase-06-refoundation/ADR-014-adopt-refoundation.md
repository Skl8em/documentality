## Status

accepted — 2026-07-21

## Context and forces in play *(justify)*

The v1–v3 theory kept needing rescue under our own critiques.
Its `formative` boolean, its force-welded perlocution, and its deferred "temporal orientation" did not hold.
A revised trilogy re-grounds the whole account on Ferraris's *documentality* — *object = inscribed act* — where inscription is what makes a decision, a debt, a commitment exist socially, against Searle's collective intentionality.

## Options considered

- Patch the v3 theory further — consequence: more rescues, the same fragility.
- Adopt the Ferraris-grounded revised trilogy as the new basis — consequence: a stronger, more honest foundation, at the cost of refactoring the product built on the old one.

## Decision

The three revised essays — `The Inscribed Act`, `A Rhetoric of Organizational Genres`, `The Documentary System` — are the theory basis from now on.
The product refactors to them in Phase 08.

## Entail — what follows

The specific vocabulary changes are recorded in ADR-015 through ADR-018.
The revised essays are brought into the source (the `foundation` layer of the producer shelf) in Phase 07–08.
The old theory ADRs (001–013) stay frozen as the record of how we once reasoned; they are superseded, not erased.

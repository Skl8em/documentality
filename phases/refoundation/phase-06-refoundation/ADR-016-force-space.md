accepted — 2026-07-21

## Context and forces in play *(justify)*

We had treated the "three doors" as three primitives, and `recommend` and `entail` as new categories we bolted on.
The refoundation is more careful.
`know` / `do` / `govern` is a **gloss** over the three active direction-of-fit families, and `govern` is a *marked*, self-guaranteeing register, not a third primitive symmetric with the other two.
And `recommend` and `entail` are not new categories but **worked examples of the extension method**: an unnamed cell surfaces as another's failure mode, and naming it — with its feature and place — is how a saturated repertoire grows.

## Options considered

- Keep the doors as primitives and the two additions as categories — consequence: hides that the space is derived and that it extends by a method.
- Present the doors as a gloss and the two additions as method-examples — consequence: honest about what is deductive (direction of fit) versus saturated (the cells).

## Decision

`know` / `do` / `govern` is documented as a gloss over the direction-of-fit families, `govern` marked.
`recommend` and `entail` are documented as examples of how the repertoire extends, not as core categories.

## Entail — what follows

The product's door structure survives, but its framing changes in Phase 08: doors as a reading gloss, cells as saturated, the extension method stated once.
The `recommend` stance and the `decide` `entail` face keep their place, now labelled as extensions.
